I'm a Research Engineer at Meta Superintelligence Labs, focused on foundation model training data for next-generation multimodal generation. I hold M.S. and B.S. degrees in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University.

Outside of work I spend my time hiking, backpacking, and photographing landscapes with my Google Pixel.
